Conclusion

Symmetrical composition shows balance and stability, top view reveals the overall layout, leading lines guide the viewer’s focus, and low angle enhances the subject’s height and power.

对称构图展现平衡与稳定，顶视构图清晰展示整体布局，引导线构图引导视线聚焦主体，而低角度构图则强化主体的高度与力量感。
